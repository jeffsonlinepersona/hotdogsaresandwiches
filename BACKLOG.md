### ~~Kingdom rules, Condimenta and Naturalia at full depth, PB&J~~ Done 2026-11-19
- **Rules adopted (in principle; may be reconsidered as Naturalia grows):** Condimenta = made (fails the lineup: extracted, refined, fermented or combined) to be applied, never the star. Naturalia = one whole food or one nature made (salt, honey), bossed around but recognizable; smashing passes, extracting a part fails. The table rule: nothing the eater does at the table changes a classification. The condiment test: phylum by method, last transforming step decides, form at order. All on the Rules page; the classifier asks the method and origin questions.
- **Condimenta:** five phyla (Crystallina, Contrita, Zymota, Emulsa, Reducta). Species: MSG, yellow and Dijon mustard, sweet pickle relish (made, therefore a condiment), soy sauce (low-sodium, tamari), mayonnaise, Caesar dressing (now *Caesaria cardiniana*), ketchup (curry ketchup), grape jelly.
- **Naturalia:** phyla by origin (Vegetabilia, Animata, Mineralia), class by part, order by what was done, family and genus real. Species: lemon (*Citrus limon*), raisin, pickle, strawberry jam, peanut butter (creamy, crunchy), steak, tuna sashimi, honey, salt (moved from Condimenta).
- **PB&J:** new genus *Linimentum* (spread fillings) in Segmentidae; jam and jelly are varieties. A condiment can join an assembled food (mayo in deviled eggs).
- **Still open:** a fungus phylum when the first mushroom arrives; more condiments (vinegar, sugar, ranch, salsa, guacamole, spice blends, olive oil, butter) and natural foods (parsley, oregano, peppercorns, fries) as cards.

### Steak frites, and combo meals as associations (proposal; high priority)
- **Logged:** 2026-11-15. Jeff is preparing the field-guide plate, so this is near the top.
- **Problem:** "what you order on a menu" would make every combo meal a new species (steak frites, a burger combo, fish and chips). Jeff's position: a combination meal is not a species.
- **The rule already half-exists:** the assembly rule's first line says side by side is "two specimens. A meal, not a food." What is missing is saying outright that **the menu-name test only names a specimen the structure test has already found; it never creates one.** A single menu line for two separate foods is a combo, not a species.
- **Proposed: associations.** Ecology names a group of species that live together without merging an *association* (plant ecologists even give them Latin names ending in -etum, such as Fagetum, a beech wood). Field guides list "associated species" for the same reason: the clownfish and its anemone live together and stay two species. Contrast the lichen, where fungus and alga do merge into one structure (the assembly rule). Combo meals become associations: they get a name and a card of their own outside the tree, listing their member species, and each member's card says "commonly found with". Proposed names: steak frites as *Bovi-frietetum*, fish and chips, the burger combo.
- **The test for "one thing" vs association:** are the foods built to be eaten in the same bite? Steak and fries are separate bites, so an association. Poutine (fries, gravy and curds combined) and a rice bowl (toppings on one base) are one specimen. Chicken and waffles depends on how it is served: stacked is topped (Apertae), side by side is an association; classify as typically prepared.
- **Pressure points found:**
  - **The Costco hot dog:** its diagnostic $1.50 price covers the hot dog *and* the soda, a combo. Proposed: the variety stays a hot dog; the card names the soda as an associated species and the $1.50 combo as its type association. "Extinct if the price changes" still holds.
  - **Condiments on the plate** (béarnaise with steak frites) stay applied condiments, not association members, per the condiment test and the table rule.
  - **Inedible members** (a kids' meal toy) are noted as commensals and never classified.
- **Build needs:** fries in the tree (proposed Naturalia: a potato would recognize its fries in a police lineup, unlike mashed potatoes), which fits the new Naturalia structure (Vegetabilia, Solanaceae, Solanum) and helps the every-kingdom-full-depth goal; a small data list of associations; "commonly found with" on member cards; an Associations page or section; Plate III for steak frites.
- **Decisions for Jeff:** (1) adopt "the menu name never creates a specimen" as written; (2) associations as the concept and term; (3) the same-bite test; (4) the Costco handling.

### Specimen card extras: history, rulings and status
- **Logged:** 2026-10-04
- **Want:** each card gets an optional short history (when it was classified, anything that moved it, e.g. "milkshake: deported from Liquida, 2026-10-03"), links to any Rulings entries about it, and a visible status.
- **Status options** (cards already carry a `status` field: "ruled", "pending review" or "species inquirenda" for stubs. Since 2026-11-12 the card shows a badge for the last two, and stubs show a notice):
  - **Ruled:** settled science.
  - **Pending review:** the Board is deliberating, indefinitely.
  - **Disputed:** an objection is on file.
  - **Extinct:** the specimen no longer exists in the wild. The card stays up as a fossil record, with the date of extinction.
- **Why Extinct matters:** the Costco hot dog (*C. f. costcoensis*) is defined by its $1.50 price. If Costco ever raises it, that variety goes extinct, and any successor is a new variety. The card should say so with appropriate solemnity.
- **Data:** new optional fields `history` (list of dated lines), `rulings` (ids of Rulings entries) and `extinct_on` (a date).

### FormSubmit blocked on some networks
- **Logged:** 2026-10-03
- **Symptom:** on Jeff's iPhone the form failed; opening formsubmit.co directly gave `ERR_NAME_NOT_RESOLVED`.
- **Cause:** the phone's network could not look up formsubmit.co at all, so a DNS filter, ad blocker, VPN or Wi-Fi filter is blocking the domain. Not a site bug: the same form works from desktop and from other browsers.
- **Mitigation:** when FormSubmit can't be reached, the form explains the likely cause (VPN, ad blocker, Wi-Fi filter) and offers "Copy my entry" so nothing typed is lost.
- **Possible fix:** if friends hit this often, switch to a service less likely to be on blocklists (for example Formspree or Web3Forms). Only `FORM_ADDRESS`/the endpoint in `js/app.js` would change.

### Photo-less entries have no CAPTCHA
- **Logged:** 2026-10-03
- **Cause:** entries without a photo send in the background (FormSubmit's ajax endpoint) so the page can report success or failure. FormSubmit only shows its CAPTCHA on full-page submissions.
- **Mitigation:** the hidden honeypot field still catches simple bots. Revisit if spam shows up.

### Photos dropped by FormSubmit's ajax endpoint (fixed)
- **Logged:** 2026-10-03
- **Symptom:** a phone submission with a photo arrived by email without the attachment.
- **Cause:** two problems stacked. FormSubmit's ajax endpoint drops file attachments, and iPhone photos (HEIC and/or very large originals) were also dropped even on a normal post, while desktop JPEGs went through.
- **Fix:** entries with a photo use a normal full-page form post, and the page first redraws the photo as a JPEG of at most 1600 px (which also strips location data). Confirmed working from Jeff's iPhone, including FormSubmit's CAPTCHA step. The email's "Photo info" row records the original file and what was sent.

### Owner email visible in page source (fixed)
- **Logged:** 2026-10-03
- **Cause:** FormSubmit was addressed by email until the form was activated.
- **Fix:** `FORM_ADDRESS` in `js/app.js` now holds FormSubmit's random alias. The "Email it instead" fallback was replaced with "Copy my entry", since a mailto link would expose the address again. A check in `tests/check.cjs` fails if an email address reappears in the code.
